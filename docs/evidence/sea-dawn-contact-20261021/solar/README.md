# Authored solar source and late halo

These are source and bounded native component/fixture receipts. They do not accept the player's 06:10 camera, the complete horizon scene, or unchanged player FPS. No permanent test, CMake target, asset, player log or save was added.

`SKY` retains one solar source per loaded current/next and storm texture set. The shared authored face mapping samples an equal-solid-angle 128×64 upper hemisphere. Four-neighbor components wrap in azimuth; a component needs at least four cells above absolute linear luminance 0.6 (`RGB^2.2`, Rec.709 weights). The strongest integrated excess above that floor supplies a weighted direction. Confidence rises continuously from the same absolute floor to 0.85; image brightness is never normalized to its relative maximum. [Actual source measurements](source-luminance.json) reject 03, 05 and 23; [selector output](source-selection.log) accepts 06, 07 and 12. The scan costs 0.087–0.170 ms per set and occurs on texture load, with no per-frame image scan or GPU readback.

The reuse owners are CPU-retained ARGB32 faces and `authoredSkyCoordinate`. The standard [connected-components facility](https://docs.opencv.org/doc/doxygen/html/d3/dc0/group__imgproc__shape.html) was considered; adding an image-library dependency for this fixed 8,192-cell traversal was unnecessary. Missing, non-ARGB32 or unreadable faces reject the source. Original physical hour-based astronomy and nonvisual getters remain intact. Native visual placement, angles and directional light share the cached authored direction, including both blends, rotation and finite-cube camera offset. Existing daytime eligibility, physical twilight color and the visible-disc elevation footprint retain radiance ownership. A rejected daytime source suppresses disc, glare, flares, road and directional light instead of restoring a second physical sun. The moon retains the prechange visual-vector formula.

`WEATHER.Execute` prepares this snapshot before binding light 0; reflection draws consume it without advancing rotation. Cache entries follow exact texture-ID swaps at day wrap and storm transitions. The [engine-linked lifecycle receipt](lifecycle.log) accepts those transitions, daylight vector/light/angle parity, rotation, repeated reflection draws, missing/non-ARGB rejection and recovery, and dark-hour rejection. The moon is component-for-component equal to the [frozen base-999c349 formula](frozen-base-GetVisualVector.cpp.txt) on the same initialized visual state: `(132.440628, 4451.03809, -2273.92163)`. The [rejected run](lifecycle-rejected-moon-oracle.log) compared the smoothed visual moon with the unsmoothed physical vector: a wrong oracle, corrected without a production edit.

The late halo runs after rigging and sea because priority −2 is unsigned. Its additive depth-disabled polygon was clipped at camera height, which can brighten foreground only above the angular horizon. The [native readback](halo-depth.log) uses the actual opaque DXT1 rope, synthetic geometry and a solid-color halo to isolate this mechanism. Finite depth testing protects the near rope but still brightens an opaque receiver behind the billboard. Existing `sunglow_z` plus viewport depth fixed at 1 protects both, while retaining the exact unblocked-sky halo color. The viewport is restored immediately; depth is not written. This is not a replay of the reported player frame.
