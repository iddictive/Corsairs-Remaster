# Putin model investigation

## Reduced-face reference comparison

The retained Kremlin reference `reference-putin-2024.png` and the actual reduced GM front/side crops `cgmodelsnet-recut-decimated-culled-{front,side,other-side}.png` were viewed together. The model retains recognizable forehead, eyes, short hair and mouth, but its nose and cheek shadows are stronger, the head/ear shape is not an exact match, and the rendering, clothing, lighting and viewing angles differ from the photograph. The second retained 2024 photograph is a three-quarter view, not a matched true profile; it cannot validate a precise profile fit. This is a qualitative comparison of offline images, not a claim of photorealistic identity, gameplay appearance or user acceptance. The lighter mesh remains a separate, unregistered trial.

## Reduced corrected recut trial

`cgmodelsnet_recut_decimate_trial.py` adapts the earlier topology-guarded edge collapse to the winding-corrected recut head, freezing its boundaries; `cgmodelsnet_recut_decimate_check.py` verifies welded topology and renders three static culled views, while `cgmodelsnet_recut_decimate_poses.py` renders four CPU-skinned samples. The model changes from 26,672 to 19,420 triangles and 16,271 to 11,811 vertex records. The inspected front/profile and sampled poses retain the facial appearance; the scarf remains stretched in extreme poses. Every body and bridge triangle-corner vertex record is byte-identical after index remapping. Both meshes have four boundary, two nonmanifold and zero same-winding shared edges at 1e-6, and no zero-area faces. These checks do not prove absence of intersections, engine performance or all-pose appearance. The smaller GM remains an isolated trial and does not replace the prepared recut package; no runtime benchmark was performed.

## Sampled in-between animation frames

`cgmodelsnet_recut_interpolation.py` reads the current winding-corrected prepared GM and retained `man.an`/`mushketer.an` banks, and approximates 25%, 50% and 75% positions between 133/131 selected adjacent frame pairs, including identified extremes. It uses the engine's shortest-path SLerp/linear threshold and interpolated root positions, but normalizes quaternions because the existing Python FK helper assumes unit quaternions; the engine uses a direct matrix path, so this is not bit-identical engine behavior. The largest joining edge in these samples is 0.073272 at man 7236+0.25 and 0.053237 at musket 976+0.25. These measurements neither cover all intervals/action blending nor establish shape or visibility; they add bounded evidence beyond the integer-frame scan and do not remove the gameplay verification limitation.

## Origin of remaining unusual edges

`cgmodelsnet_recut_edge_origin.py` compares position-welded edges at 1e-6 game units between the corrected recut source GM and retained stock `src/assets/characters/putin-phonk/original.gm`. ## Head winding correction and culling risk

The offline `render_officer.py`/RedBeard portrait renderer rasterizes both triangle orientations; prior portraits therefore did not test backface culling. In the separately registered `putin-phonk-candidate/Putin.gm`, 24,904 original graft-head triangles have positive geometric-normal/stored-normal dot products and 96 negative, while 2,034 stock body triangles have negative and six positive. The direction is opposite to the body. The original recut trial inherited this mismatch. The renderer initializes `RenderCullMode` to `CullCounterClockwise` in `experiments/native-metal/engine/src/libs/renderer/src/s_device.cpp`; the actual character technique and engine outcome are not established solely by that default.

`cgmodelsnet_recut_winding_trial.py` reverses 24,200 retained head and 344 bridge triangles without changing triangle membership, vertex data, UVs, weights, normals, or locators. At a 1e-6 welded position grid, same-winding shared edges fall from 88 to zero; four boundary edges and two nonmanifold edges remain. `cgmodelsnet_recut_cull_render.py` simulates one projected-winding cull direction: the prior recut shows the back of the head through the missing face in `cgmodelsnet-recut-trial-culled-front.png`, while `cgmodelsnet-recut-winding-trial-culled-front.png` shows the face. This CPU comparison is not an engine replay or proof of exact runtime cull state.

The independent `putin-phonk-recut-candidate` package now contains the corrected winding, and its producer verifies both prepared GM hashes and the texture hash. The earlier exhaustive frame scans, musket overlay and uncullled views used the geometrically identical pre-winding mesh: an explicit comparison confirms the triangle membership, positions, normals, skin records and UVs are identical, but those results do not prove front-face culling. **The existing integration still registers the old opposite-winding candidate**; the integration owner must replace its selected manifest or correct that asset, never register both packages. No delivery or game execution occurred here.

## Wider fitted body-weight transitions

`cgmodelsnet_neck_body_fit_trial.py` extends the preceding connected band from 0.060 to 0.120 units and fits vertices whose original weights use neither head bone against a blend of original motion and head motion, rather than dropping an original influence arbitrarily. For 45 distinct fits, held-out mean/max position error against that constructed target is 0.000979/0.020609 units. This target is a mathematical compromise, not an anatomy oracle. The full-strength result keeps sampled bridge edges at 0.006147 units, but `cgmodelsnet_neck_body_fit_checks.py` measures an affected clothing-edge increase up to 0.074802 units in 133 sampled frames. The result is rejected.

`cgmodelsnet_neck_body_fit_half.py` tests the same 0.120 band at half strength. Maximum sampled bridge length is 0.024855 in 131 frames, but the adjacent-edge scan still finds a 0.048172-unit increase at attack frame 5320. The profile renders `cgmodelsnet-neck-body-fit-half-{5320,7236,8423}-*.png` show the remaining rear protrusion and clothing distortions. Neither version replaces the candidate. These results isolate the tradeoff: fitting the two-bone representation locally cannot be judged by the bridge alone; changing body motion creates large errors at neighboring edges. Full animations, interpolation, and runtime remain unchecked.

## Body-side head-bone following trial

`cgmodelsnet_neck_body_follow_trial.py` follows body-connected vertices over a 0.060-unit band below the cut and smoothly increases head-bone influence to 100% at the body boundary, retaining the existing two-influence format. It changes 212 body/bridge records. In 131 sampled `man.an` frames the 695 bridge edges remain at their bind length, with maximum 0.0061473 units; bind recovery error is 1.07e-7. This apparent improvement transfers deformation to the adjacent body mesh: `cgmodelsnet_neck_body_follow_edge_check.py` finds a maximum affected-edge length increase of 0.082645 units at frame 7236 and 0.064244 at attack frame 5320, relative to the current candidate, across 133 sampled frames. The trial GM and renders remain isolated; they are not the candidate. Future work needs a broader continuous weight/geometry transition that preserves the adjacent garment rather than making the cut rigid. Full-bank and runtime behavior were not tested for this rejected variant.

## Adaptation boundary

The project example `src/assets/characters/red-beard/prepare.py` edits a known skinned GM and attaches a small shell using its existing packed head influence; it is not a general GLB-to-skinned-GM importer or a mesh simplifier. A head transplant needs a usable licensed source, mesh reduction, separation and fitting to the original neck, UV/material handling, and binding to the existing head bone, followed by animation and visual checks. A full body needs compatible skinning and animation mapping. This candidate's two million triangles make direct inclusion unjustified and its reuse terms are unresolved; no implementation or ready-to-integrate asset is claimed.
